# ListItem

A card row that leads with a value on a shape or an icon badge, then a title and subtitle, then one figure in the tone's accent at the end.

- `--surface-bright`, 28px corners (or the radii of a group), padding 16/20.
- The title is 14px/500, the subtitle 12px in `--on-surface-variant`, and the trailing figure 15px/700 in the tone accent.
- Use it for hints (bulb badge, no trailing), recent items, and entries in a history list. Pass `href` to make the whole row a link.
